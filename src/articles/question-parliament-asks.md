Parliamentary questions come in two forms. Starred questions are eligible for an oral answer and follow-up questions on the floor. Unstarred questions receive written replies.

Across the session, **91.8% were unstarred**. That pattern is partly procedural, but the distinction matters: an oral answer creates a live opportunity to challenge an incomplete response.

## Read the denominator

The archive contains thousands of written answers and only a limited number of oral slots. A ministry's accountability therefore depends not only on how often it is questioned, but on which questions reach the floor and what happens after the first answer.
