Seven bills moved through the parliamentary docket. Reading their titles alone obscures the practical question: who gains a new right, duty, risk, or cost when each proposal becomes law?

This briefing maps every bill to the ministry responsible, the people or institutions most directly affected, and the existing rule it changes.

## Follow the implementation

Passing a bill is the visible moment. Rules, notifications, budgets, appointments, and enforcement determine its real effect. The Chronicle will keep each measure on the board after the vote so the implementation record does not disappear with the headline.
