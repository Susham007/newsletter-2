A committee constituted through the Supreme Court process is accepting representations on the future of the Aravalli landscape. The invitation matters for communities across Delhi, Haryana, Rajasthan, and Gujarat whose water, air, land, and livelihoods are tied to the range.

Public participation is only useful when the procedure is legible. Submissions should identify the location, describe the impact, include supporting records where possible, and state the remedy being requested.

## Build a public record

Residents, researchers, local bodies, and civil-society groups can use the hearing to put field evidence into an official record. A precise submission is more durable than a general objection: name the place, document the change, and connect it to the committee's terms.
